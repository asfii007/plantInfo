ABOUT THE PROJECT

Identification and Mangement of Plant Diseases

PlantInfo is a web-based platform developed to help users identify and understand common plant diseases along with their prevention and management methods.

Our Smart Agriculture Support System helps users identify plant diseases by uploading leaf or crop images. The system analyzes the image and provides disease information along with prevention and management methods. It supports farmers and students in improving plant health using modern technology and sustainable agricultural practices.
